# Adopt the Field Almanac brand direction

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-12

## Context

The app had no consistent visual identity: generic Material components, hardcoded greens, Roboto, and repeated rounded cards. The planning documents position Smart Crops as a trusted field companion, not a generic AI utility. The existing Agri Lynk mark is bright lime and deep green, and the product must work for rural farmers in sunlight and low-connectivity conditions.

## Decision

Adopt **Field Almanac** as the product's visual direction:

- Mist-green canvas, Forest Ink text, Canopy actions, Leaf as the signature accent, Palay for caution, Clay for critical risk.
- Fraunces for display moments and Public Sans for UI and body.
- Real Philippine farm photography, custom empty-state illustrations, and a scan-HUD motif.
- Hairline borders over diffuse shadows; one memorable element per screen.

Keep the existing logo and create mono, reversed, and app-icon variants rather than redesigning the mark.

## Why

The direction ties the interface to the subject matter, gives the product a recognizable identity, and supports the trust and clarity the brand strategy requires. It also avoids the generic default of identical rounded cards and gradient chrome.

## Consequences

- All screens must consume the token layer; raw colors and font sizes are disallowed.
- Photography and illustration require a budget, licensing, and consent process.
- The palette must be contrast-tested, especially Leaf and Palay on light surfaces.
- A future rebrand would be a visual-layer change, not an information-architecture change.

## Considered alternatives

- **Precision Agritech:** credible but colder and more data-dense than the audience needs.
- **Sunrise Harvest:** friendly but close to a generic lifestyle app.
- **Keep current Material green:** lowest effort, weakest differentiation.
