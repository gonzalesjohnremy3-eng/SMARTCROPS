# Make trust the constraint on marketing psychology

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-12

## Context

Smart Crops depends on farmers trusting a diagnosis with their harvest. Growth pressure creates tempting tactics: fake scarcity, fabricated social proof, aggressive paywalls, and disguised affiliate offers. Any of these would undermine the product's core promise.

## Decision

Apply marketing psychology under a trust-first constraint:

- Use loss aversion only for real risks; no fabricated urgency.
- Use social proof only with real farmers, partners, or cooperatives.
- Use authority with clear sources and agronomist review.
- Show the free tier honestly: 5 scans per week.
- Place Agri Pro prompts after value is delivered and at real quota moments.
- Label every Partner Offer and disclose commission.
- Explain AI confidence and limitations in plain language.
- Allow dismissal without losing access to core care.
- Never pre-check consent, hide renewal, or disguise an ad.

## Why

Trust is the product's conversion engine. A farmer who feels manipulated will not return, and a wrong diagnosis amplified by fear-based copy is harmful. Ethical persuasion is also more durable than dark patterns.

## Consequences

- Some short-term conversion tactics are off the table.
- Copy and design reviews must check claims and disclosures.
- Analytics must measure trust signals, not only conversion.
- Partner relationships require clear commercial terms.

## Considered alternatives

- **Growth-first:** higher short-term conversion, unacceptable trust risk.
- **No monetization UI:** safer but fails the product's sustainability goal.
