# Smart Crops by Agri Lynk

Smart Crops is an AI-assisted crop health companion for Filipino farmers. This glossary fixes the vocabulary used in product, design, copy, and code so that a single concept has a single name.

## Language

### Brand and product

**Smart Crops**:
The mobile product farmers use to scan crops, understand problems, plan care, and protect their harvest.
_Avoid_: The app, Smart Crop, Agri Lynk app

**Agri Lynk**:
The parent brand and cooperative network behind Smart Crops.
_Avoid_: The company, the platform

**Field Briefing**:
The daily summary at the top of Home that combines weather, one priority action, and a scan prompt.
_Avoid_: Dashboard hero, weather card

### Farm and crops

**Farmer**:
The person who manages a farm and uses Smart Crops.
_Avoid_: User, customer, account

**Farm**:
The growing operation a farmer manages, identified by a name and location.
_Avoid_: Field, plot, garden

**Location**:
The place used to resolve weather and regional advisories for a farm.
_Avoid_: Address, coordinates

**Crop**:
A plant type a farmer cultivates, such as pechay, rice, tomato, or corn.
_Avoid_: Plant, produce, commodity

**Main Crop**:
A crop a farmer explicitly selects to personalize diagnosis, care, and calendar guidance.
_Avoid_: Favorite crop, selected plant

**Crop Stage**:
The current growth phase of a crop, such as seedling, vegetative, flowering, fruiting, or harvest.
_Avoid_: Growth phase, lifecycle

**Season**:
The current cropping cycle a farmer is planning and protecting.
_Avoid_: Campaign, period

**Harvest**:
The crop yield a farmer is protecting; also the outcome Smart Crops exists to safeguard.
_Avoid_: Output, production

### Crop health

**Scan**:
A farmer's act of capturing a crop image and requesting analysis.
_Avoid_: Capture, upload, check

**Diagnosis**:
The finding produced by a Scan, such as a disease, pest, nutrient issue, or healthy crop.
_Avoid_: Result, prediction, detection

**Scan Result**:
The screen and record that present a Diagnosis and its next steps.
_Avoid_: Report, analysis page

**Confidence**:
The model's estimated reliability in a Diagnosis, expressed plainly and never presented as a guarantee.
_Avoid_: Accuracy, certainty, probability

**Severity**:
The practical risk a Diagnosis poses to the crop, expressed as Healthy, Watch, or At Risk.
_Avoid_: Risk score, grade, level

**Treatment Step**:
A specific, time-bound action a farmer can take after a Diagnosis.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, tip, advice

### Planning and care

**Care Plan**:
The personalized set of actions for a farmer's crops, shaped by crop stage, scan history, and weather.
_Avoid_: Recommendations, tips, suggestions

**Field Calendar**:
The schedule of care tasks and milestones for a farmer's crops.
_Avoid_: Crop calendar, planner, schedule

**Task**:
A single scheduled action on the Field Calendar.
_Avoid_: Event, reminder, to-do

**Weather-Adjusted**:
Describes a Task whose timing or wording changed because of the forecast.
_Avoid_: Auto-rescheduled, smart task

**Health Log**:
The chronological record of a farmer's Scans, Diagnoses, and completed care.
_Avoid_: History, scan history, records

**Season Progress**:
The share of this season's planned care that a farmer has completed.
_Avoid_: Streak, score, completion rate

### Communication and advisories

**Advisory**:
A time-sensitive message about crop health, weather, or cooperative activity.
_Avoid_: Alert, notification, announcement

**Critical Advisory**:
An Advisory that requires immediate attention because of severe crop or weather risk.
_Avoid_: Emergency alert, red alert

**Cooperative Advisory**:
An Advisory sent by a cooperative to its members.
_Avoid_: Broadcast, bulletin

**Broadcast Advisory**:
A Cooperative Advisory composed by a cooperative administrator or extension officer.
_Avoid_: Blast, mass alert

### Commerce and plans

**Free Scan**:
One of the weekly scan allowances available without an Agri Pro plan.
_Avoid_: Trial scan, freebie

**Agri Pro**:
The paid plan that removes scan limits and adds priority analysis, trends, and weather-adjusted reminders.
_Avoid_: Premium, subscription, upgrade

**Agri Shop**:
The curated marketplace of products relevant to a farmer's Diagnosis or Care Plan.
_Avoid_: Marketplace, store, catalog

**Partner Offer**:
A product in Agri Shop from a merchant partner, clearly disclosed as a commercial recommendation.
_Avoid_: Sponsored product, ad, affiliate

**Merchant**:
A supplier whose product appears in Agri Shop.
_Avoid_: Vendor, seller, partner

### Cooperative

**Cooperative**:
A farmer organization whose members share advisories and regional crop-health information.
_Avoid_: Coop, enterprise, organization

**Cooperative Member**:
A farmer who belongs to a Cooperative.
_Avoid_: Member user, subscriber

**Extension Officer**:
A Cooperative role that supports farmers with advisories and regional guidance.
_Avoid_: Agronomist, officer, admin

**Cooperative Administrator**:
A Cooperative role that manages members and broadcasts advisories.
_Avoid_: Coop admin, owner

**Outbreak Summary**:
An aggregated view of Diagnoses across a Cooperative's region, never exposing an individual farm.
_Avoid_: Outbreak report, heatmap, regional score

### Interface vocabulary

**Scan Action**:
The center navigation control that starts the Scan flow.
_Avoid_: Camera button, FAB

**Advisories Inbox**:
The screen that collects a farmer's Advisories.
_Avoid_: Notification center, alerts page

**Care Plan Item**:
A single action within a Care Plan that a farmer can complete or schedule.
_Avoid_: Recommendation card, tip

**Partner Disclosure**:
The plain-language notice that Agri Shop may earn a commission from a Partner Offer.
_Avoid_: Affiliate disclaimer, legal note

**AI Limitation Notice**:
The plain-language notice that a Diagnosis supports, but does not replace, expert judgment.
_Avoid_: Disclaimer, warning
